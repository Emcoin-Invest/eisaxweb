FAQ

Common questions from operators and partners.

Is EisaX a broker, advisor, or asset custodian?
No. EisaX provides financial technology infrastructure and AI-assisted decision-support tools. It does not provide investment advice, brokerage, custody, or regulated financial services. Regulated activity remains the responsibility of appropriately licensed operators and partners.
What exactly is WealthGate AI?
WealthGate AI is a deployable white-label wealth and client portal. It supports client dashboards, portfolio reporting, digital statements, approval workflows, role-based controls, and audit logs — deployed under the operator's own brand, domain, and operating model.
Can it run under our own brand and domain?
Yes. WealthGate AI is designed as a white-label foundation. Brand, domain, users, governance policy, and regulated service delivery stay with the buyer or partner.
How do the AI-assisted features work without giving advice?
AI-assisted explanations are framed for education, engagement, and platform navigation — helping clients and advisors understand reports and workflows. Outputs are not presented as investment advice or recommendations.
Do you publish pricing?
No public pricing is published. Acquisition, licensing, and partnership structures are reviewed directly with qualified operators through the product memorandum and a structured conversation.
Is the platform bilingual?
Yes. The experience is designed for Arabic and English with full RTL support, suited to client and advisor workflows across GCC and MENA markets.
What engagement models are available?
Selected non-exclusive source-code acquisition, licensing, and partnership structures may be considered for qualified operators, implementation partners, and financial-services platforms.
