Security & Responsible Use

Security and responsible use information for EisaX technology and website interactions.

Last updated: 2026-06-08EisaX website governance
This information is provided for general website governance purposes and may be updated as EisaX products, deployment models, and applicable requirements evolve.

Security posture

EisaX product concepts are designed around role-based access, MFA-ready structures, audit logs, and governance-aware workflows.

Responsible AI

AI-assisted components should be deployed with guardrails, approved terminology, monitoring, and clear user expectations.

Data minimisation

Business inquiries should not include confidential client data, credentials, private keys, sensitive personal information, or regulated records.

Vulnerability reporting

Security concerns can be sent to partnerships@eisax.com with enough detail for review.
